About us

Welcome to
Layerism!

Building things,
layer by layer.

My journey with 3D printing began back when the technology was brand new. As a Mechanical Engineering student, I fell in love with turning digital concepts into physical reality, and it quickly became my favourite hobby.

Today, I work in tech as a Product Owner, where I spend my days solving complex problems. But at my core, I'm still that same engineering enthusiast who genuinely enjoys fixing things — even creating solutions for problems that never existed! Layerism is my way of rekindling that hands-on passion and sharing it with the community.

While managing products is my day job, running my own business is completely new to me. I'm learning the ropes step by step, so I deeply appreciate your patience and kindness as Layerism grows.

Whether you have suggestions, feedback, or a custom commission you want to bring to life, I'd love to collaborate with you. Let's build something great together, layer by layer!
